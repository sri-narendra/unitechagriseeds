About us

Unitech Agri Seeds is an agriculture company supplying quality seeds and fertilizers to farmers through a trusted network of local dealers. We work closely with village-level shops so that every farmer can find the right product close to home.

Our mission

To make dependable agricultural products available in every village we serve — with the right stock, in the right place, at the right time.

Our dealer network

Our dealers are the heart of the business. The company keeps each dealer's shop stocked, balances supply across the network, and supports them every season so farmers always find what they need.
